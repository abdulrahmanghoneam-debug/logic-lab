2. BINDING & PAGE ORIENTATION (CRITICAL — ARABIC RTL)
------------------------------------------------------
- Page Count : 20 pages (5 folded sheets — exact multiple of 4 for saddle-stitch).
- Binding    : RIGHT-EDGE SPINE (Arabic Right-to-Left reading order; pages turn Left -> Right).
- Stitching  : 2-staple saddle-stitch (تدبيس سلك وسطي من اليمين), flat or loop staples.
- IMPORTANT FOR PRINT SHOP: Ensure booklet imposition folds with the spine on the RIGHT side
  when viewing the front cover face up!

---------------------------
- Scale        : Print at 100% Actual Size (DO NOT select "Fit to Printable Area").

                 keep rich black solids for covers (e.g., C40 M30 Y20 K95) to avoid grey cast.
- Fonts        : Subsets embedded automatically inside PDF (`IBM Plex Sans Arabic`,
